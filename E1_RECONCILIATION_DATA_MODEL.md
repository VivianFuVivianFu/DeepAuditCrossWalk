# E1 — Reconciliation Data Model & Decision Lifecycle

## 1. Overview

The **Enterprise Requirement Reconciliation Data Model** defines versioned, tenant-isolated records that explain how tenant-owned `EnterpriseClause` items in an `EnterpriseBaseline` relate to external `AtomicObligation` items in the `Shared Regulatory Library` (and to peer enterprise clauses within the same baseline).

All models are implemented in [backend/banking_crosswalk/models.py](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/models.py) and persisted via [backend/banking_crosswalk/schema.py](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/schema.py), [backend/banking_crosswalk/store.py](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/store.py), and [backend/banking_crosswalk/sql_store.py](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/sql_store.py).

## 2. Relationship Classifications (`ReconciliationRelationship`)

| Classification | Value | Meaning & Validation Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Equivalent** | `equivalent` | Approved enterprise clause and approved control mapping cover all required facets of the external obligation with aligned scope, mandatory modality, timing, actor, and no weakening conditions or exceptions. Remains `proposed` until approved by an authorised human reviewer. |
| **Partially Overlapping** | `partially_overlapping` | Shares a canonical control or subset of facets with an external obligation, or is narrowed by a conditional clause, or relies on an unapproved (`proposed`) control mapping, leaving unaddressed external facets. |
| **Additional Enterprise Requirement** | `additional_enterprise_requirement` | Approved internal enterprise requirement (e.g., enterprise-specific delegated authority ledger or internal governance gate) with no external regulatory obligation counterpart. |
| **Stricter Enterprise Requirement** | `stricter_enterprise_requirement` | Imposes a stricter condition than the related external obligation: lower monetary approval trigger (`AUD 500` vs `AUD 1,000`), inclusive operator boundary (`>= 500` vs `> 500`), shorter notification SLA (`24 hours` vs `72 hours`), `dual_approval` / two-up sign-off, `pre_execution` blocking gate, or strict prohibition. |
| **Weaker or Potentially Insufficient** | `weaker_or_potentially_insufficient` | Less restrictive than the external obligation: higher monetary approval trigger (`AUD 5,000` vs `AUD 1,000`), exclusive operator boundary (`> 500` vs `>= 500` missing `500`), `post_execution` review instead of `pre_execution` approval, weakening exception/waiver clause, lower approval actor, or discretionary language (`may`/`should`). |
| **Potential Conflict** | `potential_conflict` | Contradicts an external obligation or peer enterprise clause: explicit exemption / absence of control (`does not require`), notification deadline exceeding statutory maximum (`14 days` vs `72 hours`), or conflicting internal enterprise thresholds/timings. |
| **Unmapped / No Reliable Relationship** | `unmapped_no_reliable_relationship` | Approved enterprise clause has no canonical Unified Control mapping (`is_unmapped=True`) and no reliable lexical/facet relationship to any external obligation, or has a disjoint operational scope. |
| **Undetermined** | `undetermined` | Relationship cannot be safely established due to currency mismatch (`USD` vs `AUD`), ambiguous/indeterminate phrasing (`as determined from time to time`), or unapproved clause status. |

## 3. Decision Status Lifecycle (`ReconciliationDecisionStatus`)

- `proposed`: Deterministically generated by Stage A + Stage B (`generate_reconciliation_candidates`).
- `needs_review`: Flagged by Stage B (or human reviewer) when conflicts, weaker terms, operator mismatches, actor mismatches, proposed mappings, or `undetermined` conditions require explicit human resolution.
- `approved`: Explicitly approved by an authorised human reviewer (`reviewer`, `legal`, `compliance`, `risk`, or `admin`).
- `rejected`: Explicitly rejected by an authorised human reviewer.
- `superseded`: Superseded by a newer baseline or policy version.

## 5. Relational Persistence & Row-Level Security (Alembic Migration `0003`)

Migration [0003_enterprise_reconciliation.py](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/migrations/versions/0003_enterprise_reconciliation.py) creates two tenant-scoped tables:

1. `reconciliation_decisions`:
   - Primary Key: `(tenant_id, object_id)`
   - Indexed Columns: `baseline_id`, `clause_id`, `obligation_id`
   - Index: `reconciliation_decisions_baseline_idx (tenant_id, baseline_id)`
   - PostgreSQL RLS: `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with policy `tenant_id = nullif(current_setting('crosswalk.tenant_id', true), '')`
2. `effective_baselines`:
   - Primary Key: `(tenant_id, object_id)`
   - Indexed Column: `baseline_id`
   - Index: `effective_baselines_baseline_idx (tenant_id, baseline_id)`
   - PostgreSQL RLS: `ENABLE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` with policy `tenant_id = nullif(current_setting('crosswalk.tenant_id', true), '')`
