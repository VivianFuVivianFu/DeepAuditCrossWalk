# E4 Regulatory Completeness Matrix — Australian Banking & Financial Services AI Governance

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Scope:** Completeness of the 17-source / 45-obligation / 33-control Regulatory Library for Australian ADIs, AFSL Holders, and ACL Holders deploying AI  
**Classification Scale:** `covered` | `partially covered` | `omitted` | `out of scope` | `needs additional facts`
