## 1. Executive Summary of Existing Codebase Audit (Phase 1)

An end-to-end inspection of the repository identified the following existing architecture and reuse opportunities:

| Component | Existing Location | Audit Finding & Reuse Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain Models** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/models.py` | Preserved `RegulatoryPack`, `SourceRecord`, `AtomicObligation`, `BankingUseCase`, `CoverageAssessment`, and `PolicyGapAssessment`. Extended backward-compatibly with `LegalAuthorityClassification`, `MappingRelationship`, `UnifiedControlLibrary`, `ObligationControlMappingCatalogue`, `LayerCoverageMetrics`, and `BankingUseCase.enterprise_baseline()`. |
| **Pack & Catalogue Loader** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/catalog.py` | Extended `load_regulatory_pack()` with optional private licensed standard overlay support (`BANKING_CROSSWALK_PRIVATE_OVERLAY_PATH`) and added loaders for `REGULATORY_SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`, `ATOMIC_OBLIGATION_CATALOGUE.json`, `UNIFIED_CONTROL_LIBRARY.json`, and `OBLIGATION_CONTROL_MAPPINGS.json`. |
| **Applicability Engine** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/applicability.py` | Preserved all 9 original deterministic 4-state rules (`applicable`, `not_applicable`, `conditional`, `undetermined`) and added 10 new rules for APP 8 cross-border disclosure, AFSL conduct (`RG 104`/`RG 105`), NCCP Act credit licensing & responsible lending (`ss 47, 128–131`), Australian AI Guidance (Oct 2025), EU AI Act phased rules, `ISO/IEC 23894`, `ISO/IEC 27001`, and enterprise delegated authority. |
| **Questionnaire Coverage Engine** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/coverage.py` | Preserved deterministic lexical + TF-IDF cosine + tag/domain overlap scoring without any external LLM API dependency. Made `use_case` optional (defaulting to `BankingUseCase.enterprise_baseline()`). |
| **Enterprise Policy Gap Engine** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/policy.py` | Preserved deterministic policy clause extraction, prompt-injection sanitization, and AUD threshold conflict detection. Added `gaps_by_authority_layer` and optional `use_case` default (`BankingUseCase.enterprise_baseline()`). |
| **REST API & Persistence** | `backend/banking_crosswalk/api.py`, `db.py` | Preserved all endpoints and multi-tenant SQLite/PostgreSQL persistence. Updated `/v1/coverage-assessments` and `/v1/policy-gap-assessments` so `use_case` / `use_case_id` is optional for enterprise-level comparisons. |
