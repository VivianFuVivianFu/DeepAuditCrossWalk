## 1. Executive Summary

Using the 6 failure categories diagnosed on the E3.2 Independent Holdout (`E32_MATCHING_FAILURE_ANALYSIS.md`) strictly as diagnostic examples of structural rule gaps, we engineered generalisable fixes in [`backend/banking_crosswalk/reconciliation.py`](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/reconciliation.py), [`backend/banking_crosswalk/semantic_reconciliation.py`](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/semantic_reconciliation.py), [`backend/banking_crosswalk/policy.py`](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/policy.py), and [`backend/banking_crosswalk/llm/providers.py`](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/banking_crosswalk/llm/providers.py).

No case IDs or expected obligation IDs were hardcoded for any benchmark clause. All fixes operate on general linguistic, statutory-citation, and multi-dimension validation rules and were verified against an unseen 8-case blinded evaluation suite ([`backend/tests/test_e33_evidence_preserving_matching.py`](file:///C:/Users/vivia/OneDrive/Desktop/DeepAuditCrossWalk/backend/tests/test_e33_evidence_preserving_matching.py)).

## 2. Diagnostic Category Audit & Generalisable Remediations

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Statutory Annex vs Internal Attachment (`E32-HO-04` — Annex III)** | `AMBIGUOUS_CLAUSE_PATTERNS` matched any `(?:under\|per\|in accordance with)\s+(?:schedule\|appendix\|annex\|exhibit)\s+[a-z0-9]+`, misclassifying authoritative legislative citations (*EU AI Act Annex III point 5(b)*, *Privacy Act Schedule 1*) as missing internal policy attachments and forcing `UNDETERMINED`. | Added `_STATUTORY_ANNEX_CITATION_PATTERN` and refined `_is_ambiguous_clause()` in `reconciliation.py` so recognised statutory annex/schedule citations (`Annex I–V` with EU AI Act / high-risk / creditworthiness / Article context, or `Schedule 1` with Privacy Act / APP context) are exempted from the missing-internal-attachment filter. Also guarded `annex3_5b_credit_vs_fraud_exemption_assessed` to require explicit fraud-exemption evidence. | `reconciliation.py`, `llm/providers.py` | `E33-BLIND-01` $\rightarrow$ `EU-AIA-ART26-27-DEPLOYER-CREDIT` (`partially_overlapping`) |
| **2. Permissive Omission vs Protective Prohibition (`E32-HO-05`)** | `ABSENCE_OF_CONTROL_PATTERN` matched only explicit `"does not require"` / `"not required"` phrases, missing unconditional permissive omissions of mandatory statutory controls (*"may close ... without logging"*, *"do not need to conduct ... or notify"*, *"need not notify"*), resulting in `weaker_or_potentially_insufficient` instead of `potential_conflict`. | Added `PERMISSIVE_CONTROL_OMISSION_PATTERN` in `reconciliation.py` and updated `_detect_action_polarity()` so unconditional permissive omissions of mandatory logging, assessment, or notification controls classify as `absence_of_control` $\rightarrow$ `POTENTIAL_CONFLICT` (`NEEDS_REVIEW`), while conditional `"unless ..."` exception carve-outs remain `weaker_or_potentially_insufficient` and protective prohibitions remain positive/protective controls. | `reconciliation.py`, `policy.py` | `E33-BLIND-02` $\rightarrow$ `AU-ASIC-RG271-COMPLAINT` (`potential_conflict`) |
| **3. Multi-Regulator Retrieval & Cross-Regulator Substitution (`E32-HO-08`)** | Clauses citing one regulator's deadline (*APRA CPS 234 72-hour notification*) to waive another regulator's duty (*OAIC 30-day Notifiable Data Breach assessment*) allowed multiple obligations from the first source (`APRA-CPS234`) to crowd out the waived second regulator (`AU-PRIV-NDB-S26WE`). | Added explicit Privacy Act Part IIIC / OAIC NDB topic and instrument cues in `_match_topic_obligations_for_unmapped_or_negative_clause()` and `rank_reconciliation_candidates_for_clause()`, boosted primary targets of permissive omission waivers, and enforced **multi-regulator source diversity** in `generate_hybrid_reconciliation_candidates()` so distinct `source_id` candidates both survive into Stage B/C validation. | `reconciliation.py`, `semantic_reconciliation.py` | `E33-BLIND-03` $\rightarrow$ retrieves both `AU-PRIV-NDB-S26WE` (`potential_conflict`) and `AU-APRA-CPS234-INCIDENT` |
| **4. Cross-Obligation Timing Attribution (`E32-HO-15`)** | `_extract_timeframe_hours(clause.text)` extracted any deadline in a multi-obligation paragraph (e.g., `"within 30 days"` from an NDB sub-clause) and compared it against the first regex timeframe in an unrelated obligation (`21 days` hardship in `AU-ASIC-RG271-COMPLAINT`), creating a false `timeframe_conflict=True` (`potential_conflict`). | Implemented `_split_compound_subclauses()`, `_subclause_matches_obligation()`, and `_compare_obligation_bound_timeframes()` in `reconciliation.py` (and wired into `semantic_reconciliation.py:_dimension_precheck_summary()`), binding numeric timeframes strictly to the sub-clause matching the target obligation's domain and comparing against the general statutory deadline (`max(ext_all)`) unless a specific sub-trigger (`hardship`) is invoked. | `reconciliation.py`, `semantic_reconciliation.py` | `E33-BLIND-04` $\rightarrow$ both `AU-ASIC-RG271-COMPLAINT` and `AU-PRIV-NDB-S26WE` validated as `partially_overlapping` (`0` false conflicts) |
| **5. Missing Australian Policy Annexures (`E32-HO-16` — Annexure G)** | `AMBIGUOUS_CLAUSE_PATTERNS` omitted the Australian legal term `"annexure"` and `"attachment"`, omitted the verb `"follow"` (`follow(?:s\|ing)?`), and omitted `"discretionary interim arrangements"`, allowing clauses dependent on missing internal annexures to be scored as `weaker_or_potentially_insufficient` against arbitrary obligations. | Expanded `_INTERNAL_ATTACHMENT_DEPENDENCY_PATTERN` to include `annexure` and `attachment` after `follow(s|ing)|under|per|refer to|set out in`, added discretionary committee override patterns, and added an early `_is_ambiguous_clause()` guard in `generate_reconciliation_candidates()` that emits standalone `UNDETERMINED` (`NEEDS_REVIEW`) while preserving candidate links in `preserved_candidate_evidence`. | `reconciliation.py`, `semantic_reconciliation.py` | `E33-BLIND-05` (*Annexure K*) $\rightarrow$ `UNDETERMINED` (`NEEDS_REVIEW`, `obligation=None`, evidence preserved) |
| **6. Out-of-Domain Corporate Noise & Normalized Facet Stem Matching (`E32-HO-18` & `E32-HO-07`)** | (a) Superficial character-trigram similarity on `"AI pilots"` proposed regulatory coverage on internal marketing typography/slide deck rules (`E32-HO-18`). (b) Hyphenated/inflected keywords (`"Second-line"`, `"independently challenge"`, `"independent comprehensive RMF reviews"`) missed exact substring matching on `AU-APRA-CPS220-REVIEW` (`E32-HO-07`). | (a) Added `_NON_REGULATORY_CORPORATE_NOISE_PATTERN` in `reconciliation.py` and `semantic_reconciliation.py` and required minimum 2-token regulatory domain overlap in `providers.py` fallback.
