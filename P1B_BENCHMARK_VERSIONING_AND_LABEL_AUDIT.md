## 2. Case-by-Case Audit of Retired Facet Migrations (`P1-009` and `P1-151`)

Across all 156 cases in `P1_EVALUATION_DATASET_V2.json`, exactly **2 development-split cases** (`P1-009` and `P1-151`) and **0 holdout-split cases** referenced pre-E4.1 facets that were retired and decomposed in E4.1 (PR #38).

### 2.1 Case `P1-009` (Development Split — Privacy Act APP 11.1 / 11.3 Security)

- **Source Text:**
  > *"Customer training datasets must be secured using AES-256 encryption at rest and isolated within private tenant VPCs."*
- **Expected Control:** `["CTRL-PRIV-SECURITY-RETENTION"]`
- **Historical `v1.0.0` Label (`expected_facets`):** `["security_controls_defined"]`
- **Pack `v0.2.0` Candidate Replacements under `CTRL-PRIV-SECURITY-RETENTION`:**
  1. `technical_security_measures_implemented`
  2. `organisational_security_measures_implemented`
- **Evidence Analysis & Migration Decision:**
  - The clause specifies **AES-256 encryption at rest** and **isolation within private tenant VPCs**. Both are strictly **technical security measures** under APP 11.3.
  - The clause provides **zero evidence** of organisational security measures (such as staff privacy training, role governance, or operational handling procedures).
  - **Migrated `expected_facets` (Pack `v0.2.0`):** `["technical_security_measures_implemented"]`
  - **Unresolved Sibling Facet:** `["organisational_security_measures_implemented"]` (explicitly recorded in `legacy_taxonomy_migration.unresolved_candidate_facets`, never auto-credited).
  - **Migration Kind:** `one_to_many_decomposition`
  - **Reviewer Status:** `"unverified"` (`ground_truth_provenance: "synthetic_reference"`).

### 2.2 Case `P1-151` (Development Split — EU AI Act Article 26 Deployer Obligations)

- **Source Text:**
  > *"Deployers of High-Risk credit evaluation AI systems must retain system logs generated by the AI system for at least 6 months under Article 26."*
- **Expected Control:** `["CTRL-EU-HIGH-RISK"]`
- **Historical `v1.0.0` Label (`expected_facets`):** `["deployer_human_oversight_and_6month_log_retention"]`
- **Pack `v0.2.0` Candidate Replacements under `CTRL-EU-HIGH-RISK`:**
  1. `deployer_human_oversight_assigned` (EU AI Act Art. 26(2))
  2. `automatic_logs_retained_6months` (EU AI Act Art. 26(6))
- **Evidence Analysis & Migration Decision:**
  - The clause specifies retaining **system logs generated by the AI system for at least 6 months under Article 26**, which directly addresses **Article 26(6)** automatic log retention.
  - The clause provides **zero evidence** of assigning natural persons with competence, training, and authority to exercise human oversight under **Article 26(2)**.
  - **Migrated `expected_facets` (Pack `v0.2.0`):** `["automatic_logs_retained_6months"]`
  - **Unresolved Sibling Facet:** `["deployer_human_oversight_assigned"]` (explicitly recorded in `legacy_taxonomy_migration.unresolved_candidate_facets`, never auto-credited).
  - **Migration Kind:** `one_to_many_decomposition`
  - **Reviewer Status:** `"unverified"` (`ground_truth_provenance: "synthetic_reference"`).
