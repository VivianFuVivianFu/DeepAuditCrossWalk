## 1. Executive Summary & Assurance Distinction

This Phase 1 audit distinguishes strictly between three assurance categories:

1. **Automatically Verified Structural Properties (12/12 checks passing):** Deterministic validation confirms all 17 source rows carry valid `https://` URLs, ISO `effective_from` dates, aligned `authority -> legal_authority_type` values, valid `temporal_status`, and exact parity between repo-root CSV/JSON files and `backend/banking_crosswalk/data/`.
2. **Primary-Source-Supported Findings:** Independent primary-source checks verified legislative compilations, commencement dates, and regulatory updates up to November 2026, uncovering **4 P0 citation/temporal defects** (`APRA-CPS220` invalid FRL ID, `EU-AI-ACT` outdated Annex III commencement date following Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, `ASIC-RG271` conflation with IDR Data Reporting Instrument 2022/205, and `AU-PRIVACY-ACT` omission/misstatement of enacted APP 11.3 technical & organisational measures) and **1 P1 transition nuance** (`APRA-CPS230` 1 Jul 2025 vs 1 Jul 2026 transition history).
3. **Human Legal / Licensed Standard Review Prerequisites:** Fourteen (14) open sources have `source_sha256 = NOT_DOWNLOADED_URL_REFERENCED` (summaries verified via official regulator portals/registers rather than hashed binary snapshots stored in repo), while three (3) ISO standards (`ISO-42001`, `ISO-23894`, `ISO-27001`) have `source_sha256 = NOT_DOWNLOADED_PROPRIETARY_STANDARD` and are classified **`source_unavailable`** for clause-level text pending commercial licensing via `PRIVATE_REGULATORY_OVERLAY_PATH`.
